## 300 words

Dr. Om Gurram is a dentist and health tech founder from Solapur and Pune, co-founder of OroCare AI and founder of the CastleBlock tech community.

He studied dentistry at Sinhgad Dental College, Pune (Maharashtra University of Health Sciences). He started building software while still a dental student.

Since August 2023 he has been co-founder and Technical Director of OroCare AI. OroCare Diagnosify reads dental X-rays, OPGs and cephalograms. OroCare Sim is a VR clinical simulator for training. The VR OroCare Museum puts dental history and anatomy in a browser headset, and Dentalverse helps dentists find and cite current research. For rural patients, the team built a voice interface on Whisper and Tacotron so people can describe symptoms in their own language. OroCare is now going through clinical review with practising dentists.

With Dr. Sameer Patil he co-founded ResQ (Life ResQ Healthcare Systems LLP). ResQ is a holographic desk unit with a talking avatar that guides the doctor through a medical emergency step by step. A council of specialist AI agents sits behind it, and it alerts the ambulance and hospital. Om leads the clinical side.

In March 2022 he started CastleBlock, a developer community that began in India's early Web3 scene and now runs chapters in several cities. The Solapur chapter is led by Aksha Gurram. He also co-founded DGN Capital DAO and built Callso, an encrypted peer to peer voice app, at ETHIndia 2022.

He spoke at the World Health Conference at Jio World Centre as an undergraduate dental student, and has presented at Indian Dental Association events, DY Patil University, CoinDCX events, Solana Hacker House workshops and Zo's NFT Day with GirlScript.

He builds his side projects in the open on GitHub, from a 3D arcade game on the Monad testnet to a stock chart predictor, and lives between Solapur and Pune.
