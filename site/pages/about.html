About me

I'm Dr. Om Gurram, a dentist and health tech founder. I split my time between Solapur and Pune in Maharashtra.

My full name is Om Jayant Gurram. You'll also see me written as Om Gurram or Dr. Gurram. Online I go by omg14doteth on X and theantideather on GitHub and Instagram. All of these are me.

Dentistry

I studied dentistry (BDS) at Sinhgad Dental College, Pune, under the Maharashtra University of Health Sciences, and graduated in 2026.

Building

I started writing software while I was still a dental student. In 2022 a lot of that was in Web3: I started CastleBlock, co-founded DGN Capital DAO, and built Callso, an encrypted voice app, at ETHIndia 2022.

In August 2023 I co-founded OroCare AI and took on the role of Technical Director. We build diagnostic AI for dental imaging, a VR clinical simulator, and a voice interface for rural patients. I also co-founded ResQ with Dr. Sameer Patil, an AI system that guides doctors through emergencies in the clinic. In ResQ I'm the clinical half of the team.

From November 2024 to 2026 I was a strategic advisor to PathPulse.ai.

Community

CastleBlock is the community I started in March 2022 for developers who want to learn, build and find work. It has city chapters, and the Solapur chapter is led by Aksha Gurram. More on the community page.

Speaking

I spoke at the World Health Conference at Jio World Centre as an undergraduate dental student, and I've presented at Indian Dental Association events, DY Patil University, CoinDCX events and Solana Hacker House workshops. The full list with links is on the speaking page.
