Longer bio (150 words)

Dr. Om Gurram is a dentist and health tech founder from Solapur and Pune, co-founder of OroCare AI and founder of the CastleBlock tech community.

He trained at Sinhgad Dental College, Pune, and started building software while still a dental student. Since August 2023 he has been Technical Director at OroCare AI. The team builds diagnostic AI for dental X-rays, OPGs and cephalograms, a VR clinical simulator, and a voice interface that lets rural patients speak in their own language.

With Dr. Sameer Patil he co-founded ResQ, an AI system that talks a doctor through a medical emergency in the clinic, step by step. Om leads the clinical side.

In 2022 he started CastleBlock, a developer community that grew out of India's early Web3 scene. Its Solapur chapter is led by Aksha Gurram.

He spoke at the World Health Conference at Jio World Centre as an undergraduate dental student.
