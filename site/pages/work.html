OroCare AI

Co-founder and Technical Director, August 2023 to now. orocare.live · Instagram

OroCare AI builds diagnostic software for dentistry. I lead the technology.

OroCare Diagnosify
AI for dental imaging. It analyses X-rays, OPGs and cephalograms and shows the results in a web app. Code on GitHub.
OroCare Sim
A VR clinical operating system for training.
VR OroCare Museum
Dental history and 3D anatomy exhibits you can walk through in a browser or an Oculus headset. Built with WebGL and A-Frame. Code on GitHub.
Dentalverse
A research engine for dentistry. It pulls in recent journals and papers and suggests citations, with a research assistant chat. Code on GitHub.

How it's built

The imaging models use Detectron2 with LoRA fine-tuning on dental X-ray and cephalometric images. The backend runs on FastAPI, MongoDB and AWS Lambda. For rural patients we built a multilingual voice interface on Whisper and Tacotron, so people can describe symptoms in their own language.

OroCare is going through clinical review with practising dentists. When it's done, we'll publish who reviewed it and what they checked.

ResQ

Co-founder, with Dr. Sameer Patil. Life ResQ Healthcare Systems LLP. Whitepaper · Demo video

ResQ is a holographic desk unit with a talking avatar that guides the doctor through the emergency step by step. Behind it sits a council of specialist AI agents, and it alerts the ambulance and the hospital.

I'm the clinical side of ResQ.

An earlier prototype, the ResQ AI Agent, did emergency triage over SMS with live location, using LangChain, Llama 2, Twilio and Mapbox for ambulance routing.
