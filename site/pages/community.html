Community

CastleBlock

A tech community I started in March 2022.

CastleBlock began in India's early Web3 scene and grew into a network of city chapters. We run education sessions and build workshops, and we help members find jobs and internships.

Chapters

Solapur

Led by Aksha Gurram. The Solapur chapter focuses on local job pathways, so people can build a tech career from Solapur.

Events

CastleBlock has been part of Route2Devcon, Superteam India ecosystem calls, NFT Day India, Solana Hacker Houses and Zo's AIX. Write-ups with dates, photos and speakers for each event will be added here.
